About InAmigos Foundation

Creating Social Impact Through Action & Compassion

InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization working towards education, social welfare, women empowerment, environmental sustainability, and youth development.

The organization actively conducts awareness campaigns, volunteer programs, food donation drives, plantation initiatives, and internship opportunities for students across India.

Over 250+ interns have already become permanent members of the growing InAmigos family.
